# LITTLEBIG: decisions

One line per deviation from BRIEF.md or contract change, with the reason. Newest at the bottom of
each section. Tag lines with the task id.

## Foundation (F0)

- [F0] World axes: planet at the origin, +Y = north pole, (lat 0, lon 0) = +Z; heading = compass radians clockwise from north. One convention for lat/lon, headings and charts (world/sphere.ts).
- [F0] City plan space: +x = east, +z = south at the centre, so (x, up, z) is right-handed and "right of (dx, dz)" is (−dz, dx). Plan angles increase clockwise seen from above; every outline uses positive winding (Σ x_i·z_{i+1} − x_{i+1}·z_i > 0). Documented in world/city/types.ts.
- [F0] The city chart's exponential map measures arc length on the plateau surface (R + 2 = 162 m), not sea level, so plan metres are surface metres. Circumferential distances shrink by sin θ/θ (−5 % at the plateau edge); collision and validation use plan space consistently, so nothing clashes.
- [F0] City at lat 20°, lon 10°. Off the poles (no frame flips) and off the equator.
- [F0] The planet does not spin; the sun circles it westward (world/sun.ts). Same look, simpler shadows/caches, and every lit position stays fixed.
- [F0] Tiny-planet consequence: local sun elevation changes ~1° per 2.8 m, so the city spans ~30° of "time of day" edge to edge. Shots that need a sun angle (dusk) compute the time at their own viewpoint (`eveningTimeAt`), not at the city centre.
- [F0] Plateau blend 0.2 rad (≈32 m) plus a land guard ring 0.3 rad beyond it: the plateau always sits on land, coast 30-60 m past the blend, slope across the edge < 0.7 (spec'd). The first draft put the sea right at the plateau edge (a 10 m drop).
- [F0] Coastline is continuous (height 0 at the waterline from both sides); the first draft had a 0.5 m step there.
- [F0] Camera state stores the heading as a tangent vector (`fwd`), not an angle: no gimbal flip over the poles. `heading` is derived. Drag-look yaw turns `fwd` directly, so the view direction survives lifting off from street level; `lookYaw` stays in the state (contract) but core no longer accumulates it.
- [F0] `ViewSpec` (setView) takes degrees; internal math is radians. `pitch` in a ViewSpec is an override that holds until the user interacts, then blends back to the altitude curve.
- [F0] `setView(v, { glide })` (additive): scripted sequences (the dive, C1's clip) let the ground/roof reference spring between frames instead of snapping, so crossing a building does not jolt the camera.
- [F0] (Superseded in the fix round, see below: roofs are no longer part of the altitude reference.)
- [F0] Pitch curve: −90° above 120 m, −8° at 4 m, −4° at eye height (BRIEF said ~−8° at 4 m; at 1.7 m −4° keeps the horizon just below centre). FOV 40° → 70° in log-altitude. Spec checks the horizon is in frame below ~25 m. (Made C1 in the fix round.)
- [F0] Shared fill light lives in the toon shader (planet-aware hemisphere: up = normalised world position), not a three HemisphereLight (whose up is a fixed world axis, wrong on a sphere). Fill values are ×π-scaled because three divides indirect diffuse by π.
- [F0] Tone mapping: NeutralToneMapping (Khronos PBR Neutral). Keeps the saturated palette's hues; ACES desaturated and darkened it. B4 may change it.
- [F0] Toon ramp is an R8 64-px linear texture with soft band edges (RAMP_STOPS in render/toon.ts); the night side gets no direct light, only the fill.
- [F0] Reveal = per-instance `aReveal` delay (or a per-material delay) against one shared clock uniform; the spring is in the vertex shader (geometry grows from its local origin), and the shadow depth twin gets the same code. Reduced motion dither-fades instead (no transparency, no sorting).
- [F0] Shader warm-up: `renderer.compileAsync` (KHR_parallel_shader_compile) kicked as soon as the sky exists, with a warm mesh for the shared toon variant and the shadow-pass depth program, so compiles overlap terrain generation. Cold first-visit compile was 1.3 s blocking; now ~0.25-0.5 s in parallel. (Fix round: the depth warm-up now matches the shadow pass's program key; see below.)
- [F0] Terrain and ocean share one pass of heightAt over the icosphere (world/ico-heights.ts, cached per planet + detail).
- [F0] Terrain detail 6 on both tiers (2.6 m facets: crisp at orbit, chunky at street level). Detail 7 quadruples boot cost (~250 ms); A1 decides.
- [F0] Sky dome, stars and the space gradient: the sky is measured as "angle above the planet's limb", so it works from orbit (where every background ray is just above the limb) to the street. (The altitude-only space blend went grey/lavender mid-dive; replaced by the per-ray air model in the fix round.) Stars draw at the far plane with depth test so the planet hides them. The rim glow is a back-faced additive shell (R + 46 m), faded in only above ~110 m where it reads against dark space.
- [F0] The site's CRT scanline overlay (body::after) is hidden while a LITTLEBIG canvas is on the page (app/globals.css), as for GOLDENLINE: it banded the sky gradients.
- [F0] `perf()` is a serial measurement (update + render + 1-px readPixels to sync the GPU). In headless Chromium the harness itself costs ~5-6 ms per frame, so the result includes `baseline` (an empty scene measured the same way) and `net` = median − baseline. Compare `net` against budgets, or measure in a real browser with the rAF stats.
- [F0] `?cold` (shot tool `--cold`) salts every shader's output so the macOS Metal shader cache misses: it reproduces a first visit's compile cost. Without it, repeat runs on the same machine report warm-cache boots.
- [F0] The stub city (world/city/stub.ts) is built with a reusable road-graph builder (world/city/graph.ts: trimmed centrelines, lanes, connectors with conflicts, rounded intersection outlines, sidewalk/corner/crossing walk graph). A2 may keep using the builder or replace it; validate.ts is the invariant checker for whatever plan ships.
- [F0] Quality tiers fixed per session (`low`: DPR ≤ 1.5, 1024 shadow map, no MSAA, density 0.45, no expensive post). Switching tiers at runtime would recompile programs mid-flow.

## F0 fix round (after the foundation review)

- [F0] `ctx.compile()` (additive): compiles the colour AND shadow-depth programs of what a stage-2 system just added, with those roots hidden until done; the order is build → add → compile → `reveal.slot()`. Core compiles after `init` if a system forgets. Shadow programs are warmed through proxies drawn with a render target bound and no scene fog, which is how three's shadow pass keys them (warming against the canvas, or with fog, built the wrong program and the real one compiled inside the first frame: the old 90-150 ms "shadow first draw").
- [F0] Reveal duration is a uniform (`lbRevealDur`), not a define, and fade ranges too: durations and ranges never fork programs. Program key = reveal mode + reduced motion + fade mode + patch key.
- [F0] Dev guards: a program compiled inside the first frame, or by any frame after a stage-2 system is initialised, logs a console warning naming it.
- [F0] Dispose during a stage-2 build: `ctx.yield()` and `ctx.compile()` never settle once disposed, the stage-2 loop checks `disposed` after every await, and a system whose init finishes late gets `dispose()`. Shot tool `--leak N --close-at 50,150,400,700,ready`: no timer growth, no listener growth, every renderer garbage-collected.
- [F0] Stage-1 init order: the sky first (it makes the lights and fog every lit program's key depends on), so the warm-up starts ~20 ms in; the city plan is built while it compiles. Update order is unchanged (camera first). Boot marks split `planet` / `city plan`.
- [F0] City plan budget for A2: plan + index ≤ 30 ms on the M3 (first-frame path). The stub takes 33-49 ms in the browser, mostly all-pairs placement tests; A2's builder must use the grid.
- [F0] Time-slice budget follows the display: max(2, min(8, rAF interval − last frame CPU − 2)) ms, instead of a fixed 7 ms that dropped frames at 120 Hz.
- [F0] `System.resize?(ctx, w, h, dpr)` (additive), called for initialised systems before the redraw.
- [F0] `step(dt, n)` reports `time.dt` = n·dt and `realDt` = n·dt to the single update it runs; visuals should derive from `time.render` where possible.
- [F0] Context loss: rendering stops; on `webglcontextrestored` the canvas remounts (fresh context) and the engine reboots.
- [F0] Render contract for ink (B4): outlines from depth plus normals reconstructed from depth, so no MRT outputs anywhere. Not inked = no depth write or `LAYER_NO_INK` (1, enabled on the camera by core; `LAYER_WORLD` = 0). Custom ShaderMaterials merge `ctx.uniforms` and end with the tone-mapping and colour-space chunks (the stars and the rim shell now do).
- [F0] Lighting hooks for A3 in the shared toon kit: `lbDuskTint` multiplies direct light in the terminator band (`lbDuskAt`, sun −7°…+20° over the fragment), and `lbCloudShadow` / `lbCloudShadowOn` / `lbCloudShadowRot` (an equirectangular coverage map, off by default). The v0 sky sets a pink tint.
- [F0] Toon `fade` option: a shared dither fade by camera altitude (`altTerrain`) or distance, applied to the shadow depth twin too; `ToonPatch.depthFragment` (additive) for custom fragment-side effects on shadows. The reduced-motion reveal dither now reaches shadows as well.
- [F0] GLSL: no `smoothstep` with descending edges (undefined in GLSL ES 3.0 / MSL: Safari, iPhone); `lbNightAt` and the stars use `1 − smoothstep(lo, hi, x)`.
- [F0] Toon ramp band edges widened from 0.05 to ~0.08 (≈ 25 m of ground per edge) so a band edge across the flat plateau reads as a soft gradient, not a seam. B4 may retune.
- [F0] Camera altitude reference = terrain / water, or inside the city the walking surface (`cityIndex.groundH`: road, curb, lawn). Roofs never enter it; they only lift the eye through a separate spring (`lift`: rise ω 12, settle ω 3) to keep min(2.5, alt) above the highest roof within a radius that grows with altitude (a point query below 3 m, so walking past a wall never lifts you onto its roof), faded out from 40 to 80 m. Hard floors: 0.8 m above terrain and above the roof directly underneath. No zoom pumping when a tower passes under the camera.
- [F0] `ViewState.altTerrain` (additive): eye height above terrain/water, roofs ignored; `lbCamAlt` now mirrors it. LOD and fades use it. `ViewState.alt` is the zoom altitude that drives pitch and FOV.
- [F0] Grab-spin picks against a sphere at the terrain height under the camera, fixed for the whole drag (it used the roof-inclusive reference, so the grabbed point drifted over towers).
- [F0] Pitch curve is one monotone C1 curve in log-altitude: two cubic Hermite segments with the slope matched at 4 m (spec'd), flat where it meets top-down, with a zero-end-slope bump that keeps the horizon in frame below ~25 m. The old curve stalled at −8° at 4 m and kicked again below.
- [F0] Keyboard: the page variant ignores keys while focus is in an input, textarea, select or contentEditable, and clears held keys when focus moves into one (the palette works on /planet).
- [F0] `CameraService.lastInputAt()` (additive) replaces the untyped `ctx.cameraInput` side channel for the hint row.
- [F0] Sky: the sky amount is per ray, from the air along it (density by the height of the ray's closest approach, a grazing ray seeing more path), not from camera altitude alone. Thin air keeps its hue: pale horizon → sky blue → deep saturated blue → space. Cartoon cheat: the air's scale height shrinks with altitude (60 m seen from the street, 7 m from orbit), one value per frame, so there is no seam inside a frame. The dive sky now goes (0,4,22) → (9,67,143) → (106,171,239) → (168,204,237) with no grey or lavender step; the rim shell is a thinner edge on top (only above 180 m).
- [F0] Sky night per ray (where the ray's air is), and dip-aware near the ground: the sky's night/dusk use the sun's elevation above the VISIBLE horizon (dip capped at 9°, faded out toward orbit). On this planet the horizon dips ~8° at eye height, so the sun still shows after it has set underfoot.
- [F0] Sun disc drawn in screen space at a constant pixel radius (1.9° at frame centre): no egg at the frame edge at 70° FOV. The halo stays angular.
- [F0] Golden light without moving the terminator: kept START_HOUR_ANGLE 64° (sun ~28° over the city; raising it to 78° would put the eastern half of the plateau in night at t = 0, since elevation changes 1° per 2.8 m). Instead a wide `warm` window (elevation sin 0.1–0.85) turns the sun golden, and the narrow `dusk` band drives the sunset sky. Sunset colour on surfaces is per fragment (`lbDuskTint`).
- [F0] Shots: `dusk` is timed at −(dip − 1°) ≈ −7.3° at the viewpoint so the disc touches the visible horizon; `orbit` moved 12° east so the terminator is in frame.
- [F0] Connector conflicts also cover near passes: two connectors at a node closer than `VEHICLE_CLEARANCE` = 2.9 m (bus 2.4 m + margin) conflict even if they never cross (the stub's opposing left turns passed 2.10 m apart). `validatePlan` checks it.
- [F0] Zebra crossings sit `CROSSING_SETBACK` = 2.0 m out along each junction arm (strip 0.8-3.2 m from the patch edge), never on the patch or over a connector; sidewalks split at the crossing points. Additive fields: `Lane.stopS` (front-bumper stop line, ≥ 1 m before the strip, else the lane end), `Lane.crossingAtEnd` / `crossingAtStart`, `WalkEdge.laneS`. A crossing's half-width is `width / 2` (no separate field).
- [F0] `ctx.services.crossings` (additive, `CrossingState`): `busy` (people → traffic) and `blocked` (traffic → people), per walk-edge id, written in fixedUpdate; traffic registers before people.
- [F0] `planBasis()` (additive, frame.ts): the exponential map's unnormalised Jacobian. Footprinted instances use it, so the rendered box matches its mapped footprint (unit tangents made a 16 m house up to 5 % wider: neighbours 0.5 m apart overlapped on screen). Residual second-order error ≈ 7 cm at the corners of a 14 × 10 m box (spec'd).
- [F0] Ground heights (config.ts): `ROAD_H` 0.05, `CURB_H` 0.15 (sidewalk top 0.2), `AREA_H` 0.03 above the plateau, so nothing paved is coplanar with the terrain; `CityIndex.groundH(x, z)` (additive) returns the surface anywhere (terrain outside the plan).
- [F0] `CityIndex.obstacles` (additive): features with a collision disc (`Feature.r`, or a default by kind: streetlight 0.2, tree 0.4, flag 0.15, bench 0.5, bus stop 0.6, fountain 1.6) are in `collide()`, so FPV no longer walks through lamps and trunks. `Feature.size` / `seed` (additive) for trees.
- [F0] `validatePlan` also checks buildings against every walk edge, features against buildings and carriageways, connectors inside their patch, crossings clear of connectors, stop lines before crossings, and connector clearance.
- [F0] The CityPlan is read-only and deep-frozen in development (it is shared by every engine instance).
- [F0] `render/palette.ts`: the BRIEF §3 palette as named Colors, owned by B4; terrain, ocean and sky import from it.
- [F0] `dirToChart` maps the city's exact antipode to (π·R, 0), not the city centre.
- [F0] `QualitySettings.antialias` is the canvas MSAA flag (a context attribute, fixed at creation); B4 sets it false on tiers whose post chain renders into its own targets.
