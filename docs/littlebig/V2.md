# LITTLEBIG v2: a living little world

v1 is a beautiful capital on an empty planet. Outside the plateau there are only trees, the far
continent is bare, no road leaves the city, and the only thing you can do is orbit, dive and walk.
v2 makes the planet a **world**: several settlements joined by roads, ferries and flights; traffic
that really travels between them; a space station and satellites overhead. You can **ride anything
that moves**: follow a plane through the clouds, sit beside the ISS, fly like a bird, or click a
person in the street and see the world through their eyes.

`BRIEF.md` still holds: the prime directive (quality above everything), the art direction, the
render contract, the review protocol. This file adds to it and overrides it where it says so.
Deviations go in `DECISIONS.md` under a `## v2` section, tagged with the task id (`[v2-R1]`).

---

## 1. What the player gets (acceptance, in their words)

1. **"I can see multiple cities."** From the default orbit view, at least three settlements besides
   the capital are visible, each with its own silhouette and colour (a harbour town, an alpine
   village, a farm village, an island resort; a second real city with towers on the far continent).
   At night they are separate constellations, strung together by road lights.
2. **"Roads connect stuff."** Highways and country roads link the capital to the towns over land,
   graded into the terrain, crossing narrow water on bridges. **No road ever ends in the ocean or in
   nothing:** every road ends at a destination (a town street, a gate plaza, a ferry pier, an
   airport, a viewpoint car park) with a turnaround. Where land ends, a ferry continues the trip.
3. **"It's alive."** Cars, trucks and buses drive between towns; a ferry crosses the strait; a
   train (if the terrain allows a line) runs along the coast; planes take off and land at airports;
   satellites and the station cross the sky; boats in the harbours; townsfolk in the squares.
4. **"I can follow things."** A dock of modes: explore (v1), **fly like a bird**, **follow a
   plane**, **ride a car / bus**, **satellite / station view**, **a civilian's eyes**. A click on
   anything that moves rides it. Transitions are smooth camera moves, never cuts.
5. **"The clouds feel like falling through clouds."** Crossing the cloud layer (zooming, the dive,
   a plane climbing, the bird) shows a visible falling-through-clouds overlay: wisps streaming past,
   held for at least ~0.25 s even on a fast crossing. The plane or satellite you follow stays
   visible on top of it.
6. **"The UI pops."** A POP-art × cartoon HUD: chunky ink outlines, hard offset shadows, saturated
   colour, halftone, comic bursts, bouncy but never noisy. It must not cover the planet.

## 3. The world (R1 builds it; T1, H1, V1, A2 dress and populate it)

- **Settlements.** The capital (v1, unchanged in character) plus at least four more, each on a flat
  pad blended into the terrain like the plateau (no cliffs), each planned in its own chart
  (`createChart`, like `CITY_CHART`): a harbour town with a quay and ferry pier; an alpine village
  near the snowy range; a farm village among fields and the windmills; an island resort; and on the
  far continent a second city with towers, docks and an airport. Cartoon names in the site's
  lowercase voice.
- **The network.** Roads are routed over land (slope- and coast-aware), graded and carved into the
  terrain (the terrain eases to the roadbed: `heightAt` includes it, so trees, the camera and the
  terrain mesh all agree). Bridges cross narrow water. Ferries cross the sea between piers. The
  capital gets 2–3 gate avenues from its ring road out to the plateau rim, where the region network
  meets them at a gate plaza that reads as intentional at street level.
- **Two sims, one look.** The capital's v1 traffic stays as it is (its sim is tuned and spec'd).
  Everything outside the plateau is one world-space network with its own sim (V1). Their vehicles
  never share a lane: the capital's gate avenue ends in its own turnaround, the region side starts
  at a roundabout or plaza touching it, paved continuously.
- **Airports.** At least two runways (near the capital, at the far city), flat, with approach paths
  clear of the mountains. Flights between them take off, climb through the clouds, cruise, land.
- **Space.** A cartoon space station and 8–12 cartoon satellites on inclined orbits between
  `SPACE_MIN` and `SPACE_MAX` (`world/config.ts`), visible from orbit (crossing in front of the
  planet), from the street (moving lights at night), and from every ride.

## 4. Rides, picking and labels (contracts written by the orchestrator: `core/contracts.ts`, `core/track.ts`)

- **`Trackable`** — anything the player may follow. The owning system registers each one with
  `ctx.services.track.register()` in `init()` and unregisters in `dispose()`. Ids are stable across
  frames and visits (`'<kind>:<index>'`). `pose()` writes the render-time pose (zero alloc).
  `view` says how to ride it: `'chase'`, `'eyes'` (first person; `setRidden(true)` lets the owner
  hide the body the eye is inside), or `'alongside'` (space).
- **`ctx.services.track.pick(px, py)`** — the trackable under a canvas point, nearest in front of
  the planet. For clicks and ≤ 10 Hz hover only.
- **`CameraService.mode / ride / cycle / fly / exitMode`** (optional methods, D1). `ctx.view.mode`
  and `ctx.view.ride` mirror them every frame; systems read them (e.g. people keep a ridden walker
  simulated and drawn at any altitude; the clouds keep the ridden thing visible over the overlay).
- **`ctx.services.labels`** — world-anchored name tags (settlements, landmarks, the station). The
  owning system adds them; the UI draws, declutters and makes them clickable (fly there, or ride a
  label's `track`).
- **Review hooks** (D1 adds them to `window.__littlebig` and `scripts/littlebig-shot.mjs`):
  `ride(id)`, `fly()`, `exitMode()`, `mode()`, `trackables()`; shot flags `--ride <id>`, `--bird`.
  `?hud=1` shows the HUD in shot mode (U1), shot flag `--hud`.

### Rules for every agent (binding; TASKS.md "Rules for every agent" applies too)

- **Shared worktree, shared dev server** at `http://localhost:3047` (never start or stop one).
  Up to five agents edit at once. If the page fails to compile because of another agent's
  half-written file, wait a minute and retry; don't edit their file.
- **Own your files** (listed per task). Contract additions are additive only, noted in
  `DECISIONS.md` under `## v2` with your tag. Register new systems in `core/systems.ts` (one line;
  pre-registered slots exist for some). New named shots go in `core/shots.ts` (additive entries).
- **Git:** never commit, push, stash, reset, checkout or clean. The orchestrator commits.
- **Review protocol (BRIEF §9):** build, shoot, *look at the PNGs yourself*, critique as a harsh
  art director, fix, repeat. Evidence shots under `docs/littlebig/shots/v2-<task>/` (gitignored).
- **Don't break v1.** The named v1 shots (orbit, city, clouds, rooftops, street, horizon, night,
  approach, landing, dusk) and the scripted dive must still look at least as good. `pnpm
  typecheck` and `pnpm test` must pass when you finish (the long traffic/people specs are chaotic:
  if a change you made trips one, fix the cause, don't loosen the spec).
- **Performance is part of done:** `--perf` your views; stay in the §2 budgets; instancing; zero
  per-frame allocation.
- **Report** (final message): what you built · files · contract changes · best shots (paths) · perf
  numbers · known weaknesses ranked. Be honest: the orchestrator and a critic re-shoot everything.

### R1 — the region: settlements, pads, the network

Owns `world/region/**` (new, pure TS, specs next to it), `world/planet.ts` (+ spec), additions to
`world/config.ts`, `world/city/layout.ts` and whatever city-plan files the gate avenues need (+ their
specs), `region/**` (new: a small stage-2 system that registers the settlement labels and draws a
debug overlay of the network behind `?p.region.debug=1`, which H1 replaces with real roads).

- `getRegion()` (cached, pure, deterministic; ≤ 20 ms, it is on the terrain's path) and
  `ctx.world.region` (additive `WorldData` field).
- **Settlements** (§3): ids, names, kind, style, centre, pad radius and height, chart, gates.
  ≥ 3 visible with the capital in the default `orbit` shot. Pads flat, blended, on land.
- **Terrain**: pads and road carving in `heightAt` (split base height for routing from the final
  height, so there is no cycle). The capital plateau, the coastline character, the snowy range and
  the windmill hill keep their v1 look. Planet specs updated.
- **Roads**: highways and country roads on land, graded (≤ ~12 %), smooth curves (no kinks), no
  crossings except at junctions, never over water except on bridges (with clearance), always ending
  at a destination with a turnaround. Each town's own street layout (organic: a main street, a
  loop or square, side streets; quays, switchbacks… by style), joined to the network at its gates.
- **Capital gates**: 2–3 avenues from the capital's ring road to the plateau rim (`layout.ts`),
  meeting the region at gate plazas; city plan specs and the long traffic/people specs still pass;
  the dive's landing and the street viewpoints still valid.
- **Transit graph** for V1: right-hand lanes with world-space polylines (unit direction + height per
  ≤ 1 m sample, arc length in true metres), junction connectors with turn paths, conflicts,
  priorities (roundabouts, yields), speed limits; strongly connected (vehicles roam forever). Ferry
  routes (sea lanes between piers, deep water, clear of shore) and, if the terrain allows, one rail
  line with stations (tunnels allowed) as separate graphs. Airport sites: runway centre, heading,
  length, flat, approaches clear.
- **Specs** for every invariant above, plus determinism and the build budget.
- Review: debug-overlay shots of the whole planet (orbit from several sides), each settlement pad
  from 40 m, every bridge, every gate plaza; v1 shots unchanged in character.

### D1 — the camera director: rides, bird, picking

Owns `camera/**` (new files under `camera/rides/`, `camera/bird/` as you like), ride/bird hooks in
`core/debug.ts`, ride/bird shots in `core/shots.ts`, ride/bird flags in `scripts/littlebig-shot.mjs`.

- Implement `mode / ride / cycle / fly / exitMode`; write `ctx.view.mode` and `ctx.view.ride`.
- **Chase** (behind and above, looking past the target along its travel, critically damped,
  banking subtly with a plane; drag orbits round the target, wheel sets the distance), **eyes**
  (first person at the pose, heading smoothed so a walker's turns never snap; drag looks around;
  head bob only without reduced motion), **alongside** (floating beside the station or a satellite
  with the planet below; drag orbits; a "look down" framing like the ISS cupola).
- **Transitions**: entering a ride flies from the current pose to the moving ride pose along a
  smooth arc (≈1.2–2 s); exiting hands back to explore from wherever the camera is, with no jump
  (orbit or street, whichever fits the altitude). Esc, the dock's exit, or zooming out past the
  chase range exits.
- **Bird**: a cartoon bird (toon, inked, wings flapping in the vertex shader) seen from a chase
  camera; mouse / WASD / arrows / touch stick steer, Space flaps (climb, speed), Shift dives; it
  banks into turns, levels itself, keeps off terrain, roofs and trees (a soft floor), tops out
  under the space layer; it can fly anywhere, towns and sea included. It must feel wonderful.
- **Click to follow**: in explore, a click (not a drag) on a trackable rides it; the cursor turns
  to a pointer over one (≤ 10 Hz picking). Double-click fly-to and street pointer lock still work.
- **Far plane** covers the space layer wherever it can be seen; near plane right for the eyes view.
- Until L1 and S1 register real trackables, test with a synthetic one behind a debug hook (remove it
  or keep it debug-only).

### S1 — the space layer, and falling through the clouds

Owns `space/**` (the pre-registered slot) and `clouds/**`.

- **The station**: a cartoon ISS (modules, truss, big sun-tracking solar wings, radiators, blinking
  lights, a docked capsule), on an inclined orbit in the space layer, a period of a couple of
  minutes; registered as `station:0` (`'alongside'`) and labelled (a `WorldLabel` with `track`).
- **Satellites**: 8–12 with distinct cartoon silhouettes (dish comms, weather, telescope, cubesat
  trio, solar sail…), varied inclinations, heights and periods; panels track the sun, beacons blink,
  dimmer in the planet's shadow; nothing ever passes through the camera; registered trackables.
  ≤ 3 draw calls for the whole layer.
- **Falling through the clouds**: §1.5. The overlay belongs to the clouds system and is continuous
  with its v1 veil (never a double white-out). It works for every way through (zoom, dive, plane
  ride, bird). The ridden thing (`ctx.view.ride`, the bird in bird mode) stays visible over it.
  Reduced motion: a still fade, no streaming. ≤ 0.4 ms at 1280×800. The scripted dive (the /play
  clip) must look better, not worse.

### U1 — the POP UI

Owns `littlebig-canvas.tsx`, `components/littlebig/ui/**` (new), `app/planet/page.tsx` (only its
back link / chrome), the HUD flag in `scripts/littlebig-shot.mjs`, `tests/e2e/planet.spec.ts`.

- **Mode dock**: explore · bird · plane · car/bus · space · civilian, inline-SVG icons, number-key
  shortcuts, active state from `camera.mode()`, disabled when a mode has nothing to ride yet.
- **Follow card**: kind badge, label, sub, live detail (≤ 4 Hz), prev / next (`cycle`), exit.
- **Labels**: draw `services.labels` as POP tags projected each frame (rAF, direct DOM transforms,
  no React re-render per frame), hidden behind the planet, decluttered, faded by altitude; a click
  flies there or rides `track`.
- **Hover**: "click to follow · bus 7" near the cursor over a pickable.
- **Time**: a sun / moon button that eases time forward to the next evening / morning.
- **Hints**: the v1 hint row restyled; a one-time coach mark ("click a car, a plane or a person").
- **Style**: POP art × cartoon. Ink `#1B1530` outlines, hard offset shadows, the game palette
  (`render/palette.ts`), halftone dots, comic bursts, springy presses (off under reduced motion). No
  new font downloads. The site's global `* { border-radius: 0 !important }` still applies: make round
  shapes with SVG or clip-path, or a scoped higher-specificity `!important` rule under the HUD root
  (log it). It works in the small floating window (compact icon dock) and on `/planet`, on touch
  (≥ 40 px targets), and at 360 px wide with no overflow. Real buttons, aria labels, visible focus.
- Shot mode hides the HUD unless `?hud=1`.

### L1 — make what moves followable

Owns `people/**`, `traffic/**`, `air/**`, `nature/boats.ts` (and a registration line in
`nature/index.ts`).

- Register trackables: the capital's cars, buses and trucks (`'chase'`; a driver's-seat `'eyes'`
  variant if it reads well), planes and balloons (`'chase'`), boats (`'chase'`), **people**
  (`'eyes'`: the pose at the eyes from the walk cycle, forward along the walk, smoothed; hide the
  ridden walker's head or body via `setRidden`; their dog stays).
- A ridden walker or car stays simulated and drawn at any camera altitude or LOD.
- Playful names and lines in the site's lowercase voice ("maya, out with her dog", "bus 7 · ring
  line", "flight lb 204"), and a live `detail()` (speed, heading for, next stop).
- Riding a walker's eyes must not be nauseating: no snaps at path joins; a waiting walker glances at
  the traffic.
- Specs: ids stable and deterministic, poses finite over a long run.
