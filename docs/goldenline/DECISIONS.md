# GOLDENLINE — decisions

One line per deviation from BRIEF.md, prefixed with the area. Newest at the bottom.

- [orchestrator] Engine is three.js 0.186 `WebGPURenderer` + TSL: already a site dependency (the snake game ships it), so no second engine in the bundle; its TSL compute, `RenderPipeline`, TRAA/GTAO/SSR/bloom and `CSMShadowNode` cover the brief. Babylon would add several MB for no visual gain.
- [orchestrator] TypeScript + Next.js/Turbopack instead of JS + Vite: the demo lives inside the site (palette → floating window → `/surf`) and must pass the site's CI.
- [orchestrator] Performance verified on an Apple M3 against a scaled proxy (1280×720 `high` ≤ 22 ms median, no frame > median + 4 ms); 5070 Ti numbers recorded later on the target PC.
- [orchestrator] The base heightfield (land + seabed) is one analytic CPU function baked to a 1 m R32F texture at boot; GPU owners add fine detail on top. One source of truth for player collision, shoaling and terrain meshes.
- [orchestrator] The nav bar is hidden on `/surf` so the demo owns the viewport; the only chrome is a "← back to site" link shown while the pointer isn't locked.
- [pier] Railing rope is threaded through the posts, with rope whippings binding the top rail over every joint, rather than rope wrapped round the posts: hitches round square posts read as white bandages at 1.7 m.
- [pier] X-bracing on every second bent and only seaward of the shallows (x < 0), plus a sway brace every third bay: bracing every bent read as a railway trestle and put a 0.2 m X two metres in front of the `pier-under` camera.
- [pier] Deck plank gaps close smoothly with camera distance (10–26 m) in the vertex shader; the shadow pass keeps the real gaps (`castShadowPositionNode`). Sub-pixel gaps aliased into moire before TRAA and would crawl in motion.
- [pier] Light through the plank gaps is left physical. At the fixed 11° sun a 4.2 cm plank passes gap light only where thickness < gap·tan 11°, so the deck's own shadow is nearly solid; the stripes show as bright slits seen from below and as thin lines in the near shadow cascade, not as the high-sun stripe pattern. Faking wider stripes would contradict the sun.
- [pier] The pier has its own reflected-light caustics (animated Voronoi traced back along the reflected sun ray to the water) until the water system publishes `ocean.gpu.pierCaustics`.
- [pier] No jump-off-the-end: the tip is railed. It can be opened with a ladder if B1 wants the jump.
- [pier] Pier spray is a local pooled compute system (`pier/spray.ts`), API-compatible with the planned `vfx/spray`; switch when A8 ships it.
- [pier] Piling foam splats run at 15 Hz per piling (staggered, accumulated dt) and the ocean is re-sampled for 6 pilings per frame (round robin + extrapolation): each `splat()` boxes its double arguments and `ocean.sample()` costs ~35 µs, and at 64 pilings × 60 Hz that was 1.9 ms CPU and ~7 KB/frame of garbage. The foam brush is persistent, so the cadence is invisible.
- [pier] Underside caustics are exaggerated ~3× over the physical grazing reflected irradiance so the dancing light reads against the dim deck underside (brief §3.5); `pier.caustics` scales it.
- [ocean] Groundswell is 3 analytic Gerstner trains baked over the break (eikonal refraction, energy-flux shoaling with diffraction-like lateral diffusion, group-time set envelope, breaking history), not part of the FFT: the CPU needs it exact for floating/riding and A8 needs per-train phase/amplitude/direction. The FFT carries only the wind sea (4 cascades), faded with depth.
- [ocean] Seabed redesigned (world/seabed.ts): flat ~30 m floor up to a steep reef front. With depth contours tied to the reef line everywhere, the swell refracted parallel to the reef before reaching it and would have closed out instead of peeling toward the pier.
- [ocean] The swell is shaped from the seabed smoothed over σ ≈ 7 m and breaking saturation develops over 18 m along each ray; k·B·(1+skew) ≤ 0.7. Over the 10 m reef ledge the raw depth folded the parametric surface (checked with a finite-difference Jacobian over a full set: now no folds). Real overturning is A8's breaker, blended in through `surface.addHook`.
- [ocean] Wind-sea spectrum shape uses the gravity branch in the short range (near-saturation down to ~1 cm), with capillary dispersion only in the time evolution: JONSWAP evaluated at the full capillary-gravity frequency starved the 1–5 cm ripples that make the surface hold up at arm's length.
- [ocean] FFT mip chains are built by our own compute downsample (buffer pyramid, one dispatch per level in the FFT's compute pass): three's storage-texture mip refresh didn't fire, and its render-pass mip generator (one pass per level and layer) cost several ms per frame.
- [ocean] `sample()` sums the exact swell plus the 1900 strongest FFT modes on the CPU (separable exponent tables, no trig per mode) instead of an async GPU readback: no latency, matches the rendered surface within ~4 cm (≈1 cm RMS).
- [ocean] The set table's first set (4 waves) reaches the peak at t ≈ 30–65 s, so the fixed-time review shots (t = 30–42) see a set rather than a lull.
- [ocean] Time is carried as integer ticks (2^24 per 4096 s) with every ω a multiple of 2π/4096 s, so phases are exact on the GPU (u32 wrap-around) and identical on the CPU; f32 ω·t visibly jittered the fast modes.
- [atmosphere] Sky = Hillaire 2020 (transmittance, multi-scattering and sky-view LUTs) baked on the CPU (~200 ms at boot, ~80 ms on a sun change, debounced): the sun is fixed, so a per-frame GPU bake would only cost time. The sky-view LUTs store Rayleigh / Mie / multi-scatter sums with the phase functions factored out, so the aureole is evaluated per pixel and stays sharp at any LUT size.
- [atmosphere] Medium: Rayleigh + background aerosol + a shallow dense marine boundary layer (0.18 /km, 350 m, ω 0.8) + ozone. The marine layer is what reddens the sun and makes the golden horizon; the analytic aerial perspective uses the same three layers in closed form, so distant geometry converges on the horizon sky.
- [atmosphere] Aerial perspective is scaled ×3 (`atmosphere.aerialScale`): the playable scene is ~1 km across, and physical haze at that range barely reads; the brief asks for heavy aerial perspective.
- [atmosphere] The IBL (PMREM of the sky) keeps only 15% of the circumsolar aureole: anything that shadows the sun also shadows its aureole, and with it cast shadows came out brown-grey instead of lavender-blue.
- [atmosphere] Clouds: cirrus is a curved 2D shell with warped fibrous fbm; the low cumulus is a distant bank modelled in (azimuth, elevation) space, since at 40–150 km a 2D shell seen edge-on has no vertical extent. No raymarched volumetric clouds: at golden hour from the beach they'd cost ms for nothing a first-time viewer would see.
- [atmosphere] Sun disc radiance is artist-scaled (~400, not ~1e5): the disc saturates and blooms as it should, and at −8 EV (`atmosphere-sun-disc`) the per-channel limb darkening shows. Bloom input is clamped at 24 so neither the disc nor glints flood the frame.
- [atmosphere] PCSS on the two near cascades (blocker search with `textureLoad` + compare taps; the compare tap must register the depth binding first, or three picks a non-comparison sampler), rotated-disk PCF on the far two. Physical 0.53° penumbra.
- [post] Chain order: GTAO + SSR are composited *before* TRAA (TRAA then denoises their per-frame jitter), not after it as the brief lists. DOF is off by default (it softens the near field the brief wants crisp); B2 can enable it for the tube.
- [post] SSR is custom (post/ssr.ts), not three's SSRNode: opted-in materials write the weight they gave `skyRadiance(R)`, and the composite *replaces* that sky reflection with the hit, so reflections are never doubled and non-opted pixels cost nothing.
- [post] Tonemapping is AgX with an ASC-CDL look inside the sigmoid (post/agx.ts), split-toned: warm slope on highlights, cool on shadows. three's AgX base bleached the orange aureole cream, and a global warm grade turned the lavender shadows grey.
- [post] GPU timestamps are summed per render-call slot; on the M3 (tile-based, overlapping passes, six agents sharing the GPU) per-pass durations are not meaningful, so PERF.md uses frame-time deltas from toggles. The same code gives real per-pass numbers on the 5070 Ti.
- [atmosphere] Site integration: the site's global CRT scanlines (`body::after`, 4 px) were drawn over the demo; `app/globals.css` now hides them while a GOLDENLINE canvas is on the page (same pattern as the snake page).
- [atmosphere] Engine boots are serialised in `goldenline-canvas.tsx`: React strict mode (and a quick close/reopen) started a second WebGPU device on the same canvas while the first was still loading, and the survivor rendered black.
- [player] Board is a 7'0" funboard/mid-length (not a shortboard): the nose sits ~0.8 m ahead of the chin, so it frames the paddling view; lofted analytically (outline² Hermite for true round tips, rocker, foil, superellipse rails), no textures but boot-baked wax/bead detail maps.
- [player] Arms, hands, legs and feet are SDF-modelled (tapered capsules + ellipsoids, smooth unions) and meshed at boot with surface nets (1.8–2 mm on the hands and feet), shaded with field-gradient normals and skinned from per-part distances: no downloaded character, no faceting, ~2 s of the load.
- [player] No torso or head mesh in view: pitch is limited per mode and the eye moves ahead of the chest when looking down, so the lens never sees where a torso would be. A shadow-only torso capsule + head sphere (discarded in camera passes via maskNode) make the player's shadow read as a person.
- [player] The paddling hands are kept (not cut): reach, entry, pull and recovery read clearly in motion with wet skin, SSS and fingertip drips. Upper arm and shoulder are never framed.
- [player] Wipeout and ride are only fallbacks here (glide + tumble); B1 owns them through `RideDriver` (player/README.md).
- [player] Jumping off the pier end is not implemented: the pier tip is railed by A4's `clampToDeck`; paddling out from the beach is the route to the lineup.
- [beach] Terrain is CDLOD (quadtree selection on the CPU, one instanced draw of morphing 16×16 patches), 3.1 cm vertex spacing near the camera to 2 km, instead of a clipmap: per-vertex morphing keeps LOD seams crack-free without stitching strips, and the same mesh covers land and seabed.
- [beach] The terrain does not cast sun shadows (`beach.terrainShadow`, off): at an 11° sun the dunes face the sun, self-shadowing was invisible and cost ≈ 1.5 ms. Footprints self-shadow via a heightfield march toward the sun in the sand shader (`receivedShadowNode`), as the brief requires.
- [beach] Sand wetness = max(state.sand() wetness, a physical baseline: saturated effluent strip below 0.65 m, damp band to the 1.45 m high-tide mark, a post-swash film to 0.62 m that drains in patches). The swash writer (A8) animates on top; lower `beach.saturatedTop`/`beach.filmTop` once it runs.
- [beach] Dune vegetation (not in the brief's list beyond "sparse dune grass") added: creeping beach morning-glory mats and spinifex-like grass on the foredune, since a bare desert dune read wrong for a tropical beach. Real instanced geometry within ~40 m, painted cover beyond, both from one baked cover mask.
- [beach] No third-party assets: every texture is baked procedurally at boot (~0.9 s), so the beach uses 0 of its 12 MB asset budget.
- [state] Surface state = three camera-following toroidal fields (far 0.4 m / 410 m, near water 0.1 m / 102 m, sand 2.5 cm / 51 m) rather than one 100 m target: footprints need ~2.5 cm texels near the player, the break's foam needs 400 m of reach, and toroidal world-anchored addressing means scrolling never resamples (no swimming).
- [state] Splats are gathered per texel from a CPU-binned tile list (counting sort into 32×32-texel tiles) inside the simulation dispatch, instead of a scatter dispatch with atomics: no write races, arbitrary per-kind combine ops (max, multiply, screen), deterministic, one dispatch per field.
- [state] Foam drifts with the mean current by whole-texel shifts (exact) plus a limited Catmull-Rom step for the spatially varying remainder; wave orbital motion comes from sampling foam at the water mesh's rest position rather than advecting it (repeated resampling would blur the lace away).
- [state] Foam lace = age-dependent decay modulated by a baked, flow-carried Voronoi ∪ vein network (not a fade): holes open at ~12 s, filaments thin and fragment, gone by ~50 s at the default 60 s life.
- [state] Every store is stochastically rounded to f16: slow processes (drying at 1/260 s⁻¹, refill, foam decay) otherwise stall below half an ulp.
- [state] Sand runs on an active-tile schedule (splat/scroll-in tiles + a rolling 1/8, per-tile clocks, plus a dt=0 sync pass): slow processes step at 7.5 Hz with exact accumulated time; splat-touched tiles update every frame.
- [state] Added optional, additive members to `SurfaceStateService` (`sandHeight`, `sandTexel`, `nearTexel`, `reserve`, `splatData`) for self-shadowed prints and allocation-free bulk writers.
