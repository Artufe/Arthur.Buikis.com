Gatebreak

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Gatebreak is a mobile game for Android and iOS made by Arthur Buikis, an individual developer based in Riga, Latvia. This policy explains what data the game handles.

In short: the game has no accounts and I run no servers for it, so I do not collect, receive or store any personal data. The game shows ads through Google AdMob and offers one purchase through your app store. Those services collect some data themselves, as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings and whether you have removed ads are saved on your device only. They are never sent to me. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If device backup is turned on, your phone's backup service may copy this save to your own Google or Apple account.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google collects data directly from your device. This can include:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which shows your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data.

Google is responsible for this data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (including the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your answer at any time under Ad privacy in the game's settings. On iOS the game asks for permission to track; if you decline, ads are not personalised. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Buying Remove ads turns the advertising software off entirely.

Purchases

The game offers one optional purchase, Remove ads. Payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. I never see your payment details. The game only asks the store on your device whether the purchase is owned.

Children

Gatebreak is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are requested with a general-audience content rating.

Your rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing I can provide, correct or delete. For data collected by Google or Apple, use the controls and contacts in their privacy policies. If you are in the EU, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, I will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: arthur@buikis.com.
